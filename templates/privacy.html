Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendlify collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Spendlify, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

Spendlify does not connect to your bank and never collects card numbers, account numbers, or other payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored in our application database and is accessible only to your account. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendlify loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If we add any third-party service that handles your data in the future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at privacy@Spendlify.app.
